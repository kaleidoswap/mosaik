How each one trades

LiquiDEX

maker pre-signs a 2-of-2 PSET → taker completes + broadcasts
Off-the-shelf in LWK. Atomic, no covenant. But the maker is committed the moment it signs, and the taker chooses when to take — the taker gets a free option.

Firm Tessera covenant — Mosaik today

maker funds a covenant UTXO → anyone spends it iff they pay the maker
Permissionless, trustless, no maker server. Same weakness as LiquiDEX: one UTXO per offer, and the firm offer is a free option for whoever fills it.

RFQ covenant vault — the target

fund a stateful vault once → sign ∞ off-chain quotes (expiry + nonce) → taker fills one → covenant peels the vault & re-forms it
Capital-efficient and the maker keeps optionality: quotes are ephemeral and revocable (nonce bump). Cost: a recursive, stateful covenant — the advanced build.

Simplicity-DEX options

collateral covenant + Option/Grantor tokens → Nostr discovery → oracle-free settlement by incentive
A sibling product, not spot trading: the maker sells a priced option. Borrow its Nostr relay and covenant patterns rather than rebuild them.

AMM covenant

fund a pool once → anyone swaps against x·y=k
Always-on, zero ops — but no optionality at all: it quotes through every market move, so it bleeds to toxic flow / LVR. Fine for stable pairs, not a maker's main venue.

HTLC / submarine swap

two hash-locked legs, one preimage → pay leg A, reveal, claim leg B
The orthogonal axis: this is how you reach Lightning and other chains. A Simplicity HTLC covenant can also enforce the counter-payment — combine it with the Tessera for cross-L2.

Where Mosaik should go

Today Mosaik sits bottom-left — a firm Tessera covenant: correct and trustless, but one UTXO per offer and the optionality leaks to the taker. The move is up-and-right to the RFQ covenant vault: fund once, quote freely off-chain, stay revocable. Borrow the Simplicity-DEX Nostr relay and covenant patterns instead of rebuilding them, and keep the firm Tessera as the simple permissionless primitive underneath. Add an HTLC-Tessera later for cross-L2 reach. Treat the AMM as a complement for stable pairs only — never the main book.

One covenant idea — "the offer is a coin" — spans the whole map; the axes are just how much you fund up front and how much optionality the maker keeps.
