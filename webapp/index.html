Paste an offer JSON (as printed by mosaik make-offer). It is signed in-browser with an ephemeral Nostr key and published to the relay.

Offers are discovery hints. The chain is the truth: to fill one, a taker re-derives the covenant address and verifies the on-chain UTXO, then runs mosaik take-offer, where the Liquid node executes and enforces the Simplicity covenant.
